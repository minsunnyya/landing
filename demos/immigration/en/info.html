FAQ

Are a visa and a stay status the same?

Everyday speech mixes them. Issuance and in-Korea status can differ.

How do I change status?

Electronic filing or a visit depends on current status, purpose, and location. Change is not guaranteed.

What should I do before expiry?

Use the ARC (or HiKorea permit) date. Application windows differ by status.

Can an agent go instead of me?

You may still need to appear for fingerprints or identity checks.
