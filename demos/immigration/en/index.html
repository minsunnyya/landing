FAQ

Common questions

Are a visa and a stay status the same thing?

People use the words together, but visa issuance and in-Korea status can follow different steps.

Can anyone receive E-7?

No. Job category, education, career, and employer rules apply. We do not say you will be approved.

If an agent files, do I still go to immigration?

Even with an agent, fingerprinting or identity checks can require you to appear. It depends on the status.
